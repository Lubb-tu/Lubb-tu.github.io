# Lubb — Language Unveils Balanced Bonds

**An AI-powered bilingual platform for solving and practicing verbal analogies in Arabic and English.**

Lubb is an educational web platform designed to make verbal analogical reasoning more interactive and accessible.

The platform combines Large Language Models (LLMs) with an interactive bilingual interface, allowing users to solve verbal analogy questions, receive explanations, and practice their reasoning skills through a timed quiz experience.

## 🧠 How Lubb Works

Users can enter a verbal analogy question consisting of a word pair and four possible answers.

Lubb sends the question to the selected language model, which analyzes the relationship between the words and returns the most appropriate answer along with an explanation.

The platform also includes an interactive quiz mode where users can practice verbal analogies under different time limits and difficulty levels.

## 🔬 Research Background

Lubb was developed as part of the graduation research project:

**“Can Machines Think Like Us? The Ability of Large Language Models to Pass Verbal Analogical Reasoning in Aptitude Tests.”**

The research investigates the ability of Large Language Models to solve verbal analogy questions in both Arabic and English.

It explores different prompting strategies and evaluates model performance across multiple verbal analogy datasets.

Lubb translates this research into an interactive educational experience where users can experiment with AI-powered analogical reasoning.
